Play **Warcraft I: Remastered** and **Warcraft II: Remastered** on Apple Silicon Macs, with optional mods for both games on Mac and Windows.

**Mac:** download `Warcraft-for-Mac.zip`, unzip, open the app (first time: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway), click Install.
**Windows:** download `W1R-Mods-Installer.exe`, run it, pick Warcraft I or Warcraft II, click Install, play from Battle.net.
**By hand (no installer):** copy `VERSION.dll` and `w1r_mods.ini` (Warcraft I) or `w2r_mods.ini` (Warcraft II) into the game's `x86` folder.

Mods: middle-mouse drag panning · A* pathfinding · crash fix for some saves (Warcraft I). Each is optional.

Verify your download: see "Verify your download" in the README (`SHA256SUMS.txt`, build attestations, reproducible builds).

Unofficial fan project, not affiliated with Blizzard Entertainment. Needs your own copy of the game. Written for Warcraft I: Remastered build 1.0.0.2762 and Warcraft II: Remastered build 1.0.2.2818.
